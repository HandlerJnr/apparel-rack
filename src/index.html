Studio

Two people · one press · one rail

We print ten pieces at a time, hang them on one rail, and take each one down when it sells out.

Every piece is screen-printed by hand on heavyweight organic cotton, washed once so it won't shrink on you, folded, and posted in a recycled mailer. Something new goes up on the rail every Friday.

Help

Replies within one working day

Delivery

UK orders ship within two working days by tracked post: £3.95, or free over £60. Everything is washed and folded before it goes in the mailer.

Returns

Send anything back within 30 days, unworn and with its tag on, and we refund you in full. UK returns are free.

Sizing

Our tees are cut boxy, so size down if you like a regular fit. Each piece's page says how it fits, and every size shows how many are left.

Care

Wash cold and inside out, dry flat, and don't iron the print. It'll last years.
